Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and username. When you create a project, we store the project name, due date, weightage and any assignment files you upload.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the fourpointo service. We use your email and username to identify your account. Your assignment files are processed to generate tasks and extract marking rubrics.

Third parties

Assignment text extracted from your uploaded files is sent to Groq (groq.com) to generate tasks and rubrics using AI. We do not control how Groq handles this data. Do not upload files containing sensitive personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in. These are essential cookies required for the service to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Data storage

Your data is stored on a private server. We do not sell or share your data with any third party except as described above.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and data by contacting us.
